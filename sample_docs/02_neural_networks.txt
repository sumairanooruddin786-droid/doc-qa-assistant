A neural network is a model made of layers of connected units called neurons. Each neuron multiplies its inputs by weights, adds a bias, and passes the result through an activation function such as ReLU or sigmoid. Training uses backpropagation, which computes how much each weight contributed to the error, and gradient descent, which nudges the weights to reduce that error. Deep learning refers to networks with many layers. Convolutional neural networks are strong at images because they detect local patterns like edges and shapes. Transformers use an attention mechanism to weigh how relevant each word in a sequence is to every other word, and they power modern large language models. Training deep networks usually needs a GPU because of the large number of matrix operations.
