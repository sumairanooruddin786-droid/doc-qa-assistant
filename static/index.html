Knowledge Assistant

Upload documents, then search or ask questions about them.

    Distance is cosine distance (lower means closer). Similarity is 1 minus distance.

    Keyword search

    Matches the exact words you type. "forgot my login" will miss a passage that says "reset your password", because the words differ.

    Semantic search

    Turns text into embeddings and matches meaning. Synonyms and paraphrases land close together in vector space, so the right passage is found without shared words.
